## Full description

Your service year, your choices, your PALAVA!

Step into a Nigerian NYSC-inspired life adventure set across Oyo and Osun. From orientation camp to your Place of Primary Assignment (PPA), build your story through decisions, friendships, work, money management and community life.

FEATURES
- Explore a story-driven NYSC-inspired adventure.
- Experience camp activities and make choices that shape your journey.
- Discover fictional PPA placements across schools, offices, design studios, ICT, healthcare, media, agriculture and more.
- Manage your in-game allowance, transport, accommodation and side hustles.
- Build skills and reputation as you progress through your service year.
- Meet other players in the community hub and chat.
- Save progress to a PALAVA account where server storage is available.
- Earn PALAVA game-completion souvenirs.

PALAVA is an independent entertainment game. It is not an official NYSC app and is not affiliated with NYSC or any government agency. PPA names and organizations may be fictional. PALAVA certificates are game souvenirs, not official NYSC documents.

An internet connection is needed for accounts and online community features. Availability of saved progress may be affected while the service is in development.
