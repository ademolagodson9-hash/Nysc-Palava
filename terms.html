PALAVA Terms of Use

Last updated: 10 October 2026

PALAVA is an independent NYSC-inspired entertainment game, not an official NYSC service and not affiliated with the Nigerian government.

Game content

Organizations, PPA placements, characters, events, rewards, allowances, and certificates may be fictional or simulated. They do not represent official vacancies, government benefits, or real NYSC decisions.

Accounts and conduct

Keep your password private. Do not impersonate others, harass players, post illegal content, or share sensitive personal information in chat. Access may be limited to protect the service and community.

Availability and saves

PALAVA is an early-stage project provided as-is. Features may change, fail, or become temporarily unavailable. Until persistent storage is fully deployed and verified, game progress and accounts may be lost on service restart. Do not rely on the game for important records.

Certificates

Any certificate issued by PALAVA is a game souvenir only and is not a qualification, service certificate, or document issued by NYSC or a government agency.

Contact

For questions, use the project GitHub repository. See the Privacy Policy.
