PALAVA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

PALAVA is an independent NYSC-themed adventure game. It is not operated by or affiliated with the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC), Google, or any Nigerian government agency.

Information the game may process

  • Account username and display name.
  • A password verifier derived from your password. PALAVA does not intentionally store your plain-text password.
  • Game progress and game summary details you save.
  • Messages you post to the community chat.
  • Game certificate serial numbers and related in-game completion details.
  • Basic technical request information processed by the hosting provider to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

Information is used to operate sign-in, load game progress, display the community hub, and issue and verify in-game completion souvenirs. We do not sell personal information.

Hosting and retention

The game is hosted using third-party infrastructure. During this early prototype stage, account and game data may be stored in temporary server memory and can be lost when the service restarts. Do not use PALAVA to store sensitive information. Hosting providers may process technical logs under their own terms.

Public content

Community chat messages and usernames may be visible to other players. Do not post passwords, phone numbers, addresses, financial information, or other sensitive data.

Your choices and account deletion

You can request deletion of an authenticated PALAVA account and its associated in-game data using the Account Deletion page. You must be signed in to the account you want to delete. Some technical logs may be retained by the hosting provider according to its policies.

Children

PALAVA is a general-audience game, but users should not submit personal or sensitive information in usernames or chat. Parents and guardians should supervise younger players.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as PALAVA evolves. For questions or privacy concerns, contact the project through the PALAVA GitHub repository.

Important: PALAVA certificates are game souvenirs, not official NYSC documents. PPA organizations and placements in the game may be fictional.
